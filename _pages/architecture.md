## High-Level Architecture for Founder Website & Sharing

This site can be managed as a modular system:

### Layer 1 — Identity Layer
- Homepage (founder positioning)
- Profile, links, and brand anchors

### Layer 2 — Authority Layer
- Achievements and outcomes
- Case studies and published insights

### Layer 3 — System Layer
- Frameworks, operating model, and methods
- Reusable templates and decision tools

### Layer 4 — Distribution Layer
- Social channels, newsletters, and external platforms
- Content repurposing workflow for consistent visibility

### Layer 5 — Conversion Layer
- Contact and collaboration pathways
- Partnership and speaking opportunities

---

## Suggested Information Flow

1. Visitor lands on homepage and understands your positioning in under 10 seconds.
2. Visitor validates credibility via achievements and case studies.
3. Visitor explores your systems/process pages for depth.
4. Visitor selects a collaboration path (contact, partner, advisor, speaking).

---

## Update Cadence

- **Weekly:** Update active priorities and new wins.
- **Monthly:** Add one case study or insight.
- **Quarterly:** Refresh positioning, architecture, and featured achievements.

A website run this way becomes a **living founder system**, not a static profile.
